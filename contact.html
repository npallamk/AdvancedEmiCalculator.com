🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Advanced EMI Calculator ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit advancedemicalculator.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. All loan calculations are performed locally in your browser and no financial data is transmitted to our servers. We may collect anonymous usage data through third-party analytics to improve the tool.

3. Cookies & Advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand site traffic. These services collect anonymised data such as page views, device type, and geographic region. No personal data is shared.

5. Data Security

Since all calculations are client-side and we collect no personal financial data, your loan details remain entirely private on your device.

6. External Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at nithin.kumar.contact@zohomail.in.

Common Support Questions

Is the calculator accurate? — Yes, our calculations use the standard EMI formula (reducing balance method) and produce day-accurate amortisation schedules. Minor differences from bank statements may occur due to rounding conventions.

Can I save my calculations? — Yes! Use the Export button to save your loan configuration as a JSON file, or the Share button to generate a shareable link. You can also save the amortisation schedule as a PDF.

Do you store my data? — No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We never collect, store, or transmit your financial data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
