About Advanced EMI Calculator

A simple, powerful EMI calculator for smarter loan planning.

Advanced EMI Calculator is a free, powerful home loan planning tool built specifically for Indian borrowers. Whether you're a first-time home buyer exploring your options or an existing borrower looking to optimise your repayment strategy, this calculator helps you make informed decisions — backed by accurate, real-time numbers.

Unlike basic EMI calculators offered by banks, our tool goes far beyond a simple principal-rate-tenure calculation. You can simulate lump-sum and recurring prepayments to see exactly how much interest you'll save. You can model floating rate changes — what happens when RBI cuts or hikes the repo rate? And you can set custom EMI amounts to test different repayment scenarios, all visualised in a detailed day-accurate amortisation schedule.

Every calculation runs 100% in your browser. We never collect, store, or transmit any of your financial data. There are no sign-ups, no accounts, and no tracking. Your loan details remain entirely private on your device.

How to Use

Simply enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure using the sliders or input fields on the home page. The calculator instantly shows your EMI, total interest, and a complete repayment breakdown. Open Advanced Options to add prepayments, rate changes, or custom EMI schedules — and watch how each change impacts your total cost in real time.

Who Is This For?

This tool is designed for anyone in India planning a home loan — whether you're comparing rates across SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, or other banks, evaluating how much you can afford on your salary, or deciding between reducing EMI vs. reducing tenure after a prepayment. It's also useful for existing borrowers who want to understand the impact of RBI rate changes on their monthly outflow.

Built with care by a team passionate about making financial planning accessible, transparent, and completely free.
